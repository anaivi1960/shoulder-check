**Title** (68 characters):

Shoulder Check: Train Your Scanning Habit on Snap Spectacles (AR Lens)

**Description:**

Every coach shouts it: "Check your shoulder!" Shoulder Check is a Snap Spectacles Lens that trains midfielders to scan the pitch before the ball arrives, because turning your head is the whole skill.

You stand as a central midfielder on a full-size pitch with 22 players. Your centre-back plays the ball in, defenders run to mark your teammates, and exactly one teammate is left free. The glasses' narrow view hides everyone behind you until you actually turn your head, just like a real pitch. When the ball arrives, play freezes mid-stride and every player turns grey. Pinch the free man from memory.

What you'll see:
0:00 Round 1: no shoulder checks, too slow
0:19 Round 2: check left, check right, free man found
0:30 Round 3: only one look, picked a marked teammate
0:42 Round 4: turning into trouble, picked an opponent
0:53 Rounds 5–8: scanning every pass, levelling up
1:41 Wrap-up

How it works:
• Scans are measured from real head direction: 60°+ away and back within 1.5 s
• Feedback is pinned on the players themselves (FREE MAN, MARKED, OPPONENT), not in a menu in front of your face
• Each level adds a player and speeds up the pass

Built for the Learn AR with SPECS hackathon with Lens Studio 5.24 (SPECS 27), TypeScript, Spectacles Interaction Kit, LEAF tests and Blender. Recorded in the Lens Studio preview. Head turns and picks are scripted for this demo. Logic is proven by 8/8 automated tests; the feel on real glasses is still to be tested.

Project + source: https://github.com/anaivi1960/shoulder-check
